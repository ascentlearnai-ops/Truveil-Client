2Review and consent

What is shared during this interview

Microphone transcript

Only your selected microphone is transcribed, not system or call audio. Other voices audible to that microphone may still be captured. Continues while you use other calling apps. End the session or close Truveil Secure to stop. Raw audio is not kept in the report.

Optional browser sharing

Only the browser window you explicitly connect is shared. Domain names and tab changes are visible, never page contents, titles, or full URLs. Other windows and apps are not monitored.

No file access

Truveil Secure never reads your files, clipboard, or screen.

What this does not do

No camera. No screen recording. No file or clipboard access. No automatic hiring decision.

You can end the session when your interview is complete.
