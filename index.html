The candidate app shares session evidence with your interviewer. You see the notice and allowed-tool policy before monitoring starts.

BEFORE YOU JOIN

Know the rules.

Review allowed apps, websites, and restricted AI tools. Ask about accommodations or tools you need before you consent.

DURING YOUR INTERVIEW

Know what is shared.

Microphone transcript text, foreground app names, window titles, and available browser destinations can be shared during your session.

ON YOUR COMPUTER

Clear boundaries.
Before you begin.

Truveil Secure supports your interviewer's tool policy. It is not a replacement for the conversation, and it does not decide whether you are hired.

Read the full collection notice
  1. 01

    Your microphone becomes a transcript

    Audio is used for live transcription. Raw audio is not included in the interviewer report.

  2. 02

    Foreground activity becomes context

    The active app, window title, and browser host or URL may be visible to the interviewer when Windows makes them available. This is not a screen recording.

  3. 03

    Restricted AI tools may be closed

    Truveil may close or refocus away from known restricted AI tools. These Windows controls are best effort. Other app switches are observed, not universally blocked.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST HELLO

A few things
worth knowing.

Need a hand?
Do I need to create an account?

No. Your interviewer sends a session code. Use that code in Truveil Secure for Windows and complete the consent and microphone checks.

What if I don't want to consent?

Speak with your interviewer before the session. You can choose not to start monitoring and discuss an alternative interview arrangement.

Does Truveil replace Zoom, Teams, or Meet?

No. Keep using your normal meeting app. Truveil Secure runs alongside it and follows the policy your interviewer shares.

I haven't received an invitation yet.

Ask your interviewer for the TRV code. If you're interested in future availability, join the waitlist and we'll send product updates.
