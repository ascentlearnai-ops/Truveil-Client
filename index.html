What stays outside the session.

01

No camera or video

Truveil Secure does not use your camera and does not record your screen.

02

It does not read your documents, messages, clipboard, or browser history.

03

You stay in control.

Consent is collected in the app before monitoring begins. You can end the session at any time. Truveil provides evidence to a human reviewer and does not make the hiring decision.

Candidate questions

Know before you install.

Does Truveil record my screen?+

No. Truveil records timestamped session events, not screen video.

Does it use my camera?+

No camera video is collected or used.

What happens to microphone audio?+

The microphone feeds the live transcript. Fallback audio is temporary and deleted after transcription; raw audio is not included in the review report.

Can Truveil decide whether I pass?+

No. Truveil does not automatically decide, rank, reject, or score you as a person. A human reviews the session record.

Can I stop the session?+

Yes. You can end the session from Truveil Secure at any time.
