Your checklist is saved on this device.

  1. 01

    Install the Windows app.

    Download Truveil Secure on the Windows computer you will use for your interview. Open the installer and follow its setup steps.

    Download for WindowsVerify download
  2. 02

    Find your invitation.

    Your interviewer will share a code beginning with TRV-. Keep it nearby. You do not need to create an account or join the waitlist to attend an invited interview.

    Enter your interview code
  3. 03

    Check the details.

    Read the collection notice and allowed-tool policy inside the app. Microphone transcription and foreground activity are shared during a consented session. Known restricted AI tools may be closed or refocused. Ask your interviewer about unclear rules or accommodations before you start.

    What Truveil can and cannot see
  4. 04

    Get comfortable.

    Plug in your headphones, choose the right microphone, and check your connection. Give yourself a few minutes before the interview begins.

You are all set.
